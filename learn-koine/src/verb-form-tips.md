# Verb Practice Tips coverage

The Help Tool Tips section contains the hand-built present, imperfect, aorist, future, perfect, and pluperfect indicative paradigms, including active and middle/passive forms where listed, plus lexicon-derived grids for other attested finite verb combinations.

## Included

- Verb forms with an attested person and number in `wordUsages`.
- Indicative, subjunctive, and imperative forms accepted by the Verb Practice menu and its validation rules.
- Active, middle, passive, and middle/passive voice labels found in the lexicon. Deponent forms are excluded.
- Second aorist and other second-form tense labels where they occur in the lexicon.

Each generated grid uses forms from the same Strong's lemma where available. Cells without an attested form for that lemma display a dash.

## Still not included

- Participles
- Infinitives
- Optatives, which are not offered by the Verb Practice menu
- Need contraction verb forms in Help Tooltips
- Figure our Liquid Verbs
- Need to add μι verbs for the help tooltips.
