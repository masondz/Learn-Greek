# Verb Practice Tips coverage

The Help Tool Tips section currently shows hand-built representative paradigms. It does not generate additional paradigms from the lexicon.

## Included

### Indicative

- Present active and middle/passive
- Imperfect active and middle/passive
- Future active, middle, and passive
- First aorist active, middle, and passive
- Second aorist active and middle, illustrated with λαμβάνω
- Perfect active and middle/passive
- Pluperfect active

### Subjunctive

- Present active and middle/passive
- First aorist active, middle, and passive

### Imperative

- Present active and middle/passive
- First aorist active

## Not included

- Pluperfect middle/passive indicative
- Second aorist subjunctive paradigms
- Aorist middle/passive and perfect imperative paradigms
- Participles and infinitives
- Optatives
- Contracted verbs, liquid verbs, and μι-verbs
