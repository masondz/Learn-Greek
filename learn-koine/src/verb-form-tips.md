# Verb Practice tips coverage

This inventory compares the verb forms in the Verb Practice selection menu with the paradigms currently shown in the Help Tool's Tips section. The menu is the source of truth for what this app currently offers.

## Already shown in Tips

The tips currently show six **active indicative** paradigms, each with first, second, and third person in singular and plural:

- Present active indicative
- Future active indicative
- Aorist active indicative
- Imperfect active indicative
- Perfect active indicative
- Pluperfect active indicative

The paradigms visually distinguish the augment or reduplication from the stem and the personal ending.

## Available in Verb Practice, not yet shown in Tips

### Other voices

- Middle
- Passive
- Deponent (available as a voice choice; its forms may be represented by middle or passive forms)

### Other moods

- Subjunctive
- Imperative

### Other verb types

- Participles
- Infinitives

### Remaining forms and distinctions

- Any supported combinations of the six menu tenses with middle, passive, or deponent voice
- Any supported subjunctive and imperative tense/voice combinations
- Participle forms, including their tense, voice, case, number, and gender distinctions where represented by the app
- Infinitive forms, including their tense and voice distinctions where represented by the app
- A concise explanation of how person and number are expressed by finite verb endings; the existing tables show these labels, but the Tips section does not explain them

## Menu options for reference

The Verb Practice menu currently lists:

- **Types:** Verb, Participle, Infinitive
- **Tenses:** Present, Aorist, Future, Perfect, Imperfect, Pluperfect
- **Voices:** Active, Middle, Passive, Deponent
- **Moods:** Indicative, Subjunctive, Imperative
- **Persons:** First, Second, Third
- **Numbers:** Singular, Plural

The menu does not list the optative mood, so it is outside this coverage list. Some combinations are restricted by the app's practice logic or may not be present in its verb data; add tips for combinations the app actually supports rather than assuming every possible combination exists.
